Felipe Lobel

I combine theory and large administrative data to study how corporate behavior and public policy interact in the presence of market failures. Building on this, my recent work examines business taxation, cash transfers, and housing policy. I completed my PhD in Economics at UC Berkeley.
Current and upcoming appointments:
Duke University, Assistant Professor of Economics (2026-)
Stanford University, SIEPR and GSB Postdoctoral Fellow (2025-2026)
